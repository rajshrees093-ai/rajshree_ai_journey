Methodology ▸

Cost of living combines each country's Numbeo national average monthly cost (excluding rent) with a locally-reported average one-bedroom, city-center rent, to produce one comparable "total monthly cost" figure per city. Lower cost scores higher.

Internet speed uses Ookla Speedtest Global Index median fixed-broadband download speed where a specific 2025–2026 country figure could be verified (Spain). Where a precise current figure could not be confirmed in this research (Portugal, Germany), a directional estimate is used and clearly flagged. Georgia's figure is a city-level estimate from a digital-nomad guide, not the primary Ookla dataset, and is flagged accordingly.

Visa/residency ease is a composite 0–100 score built from each country's actual remote-worker visa pathway: minimum income threshold, processing time, and bureaucratic complexity, as described in 2026 immigration guides.

Climate & quality of life uses verified 30-year average annual sunshine hours per city as the primary comparable metric, cross-referenced against Numbeo's 2025 Quality of Life ranking for regional context.

Conflicts between sources, and how they were resolved ▸
Tbilisi cost of living — a wide range depending on the source

Sources ranged from a "budget-conscious" baseline of about $814/month including rent (livingcostindex.com) to a more lifestyle-inclusive $900–$1,400/month range (GREM 2026 expat guide), with one nomad-guide citing $1,190–$1,904/month for a "comfortable" single lifestyle. Post-2022 inflation means older "$600/month" blog claims are now out of date. This tool uses the GREM mid-range estimate ($1,150/month) as the most representative figure for a remote worker seeking a comfortable, not bare-minimum, lifestyle.

Valencia's total cost had to be derived, not looked up directly

Numbeo publishes Valencia's Cost of Living Index (≈50, NYC=100) but no single-figure "total monthly cost" comparable to the country-level Portugal/Germany numbers. This tool derived Valencia's estimated total by applying a sourced relative comparison (Berlin reported as ~33% more expensive than Valencia on a rent-inclusive basis, per a 2026 relocation guide) against Berlin's own sourced total. This is flagged as a derived estimate, not a direct citation.

Internet speed — Portugal and Germany lacked a precise, current, citable national figure

Multiple 2025–2026 internet-speed rankings were checked, but none in this research returned an exact, attributable Ookla Speedtest figure for Portugal or Germany at the time of writing (only Spain's figure — 248.12 Mbps, global rank 14 — was directly confirmed). Rather than fabricate precision, both are shown as flagged estimates positioned relative to known regional patterns.

Germany has no dedicated "digital nomad visa"

Unlike Portugal, Spain, and Georgia, Germany does not offer a purpose-built remote-work visa. Guides use the Freiberufler (freelance) residence permit as the closest comparable pathway, but it is generally reported as more document-heavy and less standardized than the other three countries' programs — reflected in a lower visa-ease score.
